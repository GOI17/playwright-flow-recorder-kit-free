Privacy notice

This notice explains how Alfred Labs, operated by Jose Gilberto Olivas Ibarra in Mexico City, Mexico, handles information related to this website, product support, purchases, and the Playwright Flow Recorder Kit.

Effective and last updated: August 19, 2026

Information collected on this website

This website has no Alfred Labs account system, embedded payment form, first-party analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies. If you email Alfred Labs, we receive your email address, name, message, and any information you choose to include so we can respond.

GitHub hosts this public website. GitHub may process technical service data, including IP addresses and request information, under its own privacy statement and GitHub Pages data-collection notice.

Recorder data

The version 0.1 extension is local-first. Recorded journeys are stored in Chrome storage on the user’s device. Alfred Labs does not operate a backend that receives recordings, page content, exported files, or browsing history, and the product has no mandatory telemetry.

The extension requests access to the active HTTP or HTTPS page only when needed to record or replay a journey. Users decide when recording starts, what page is recorded, what is exported, and with whom an export is shared.

Recognizable password, email, and token-like inputs may be replaced with named placeholders, but automated redaction cannot guarantee that every secret or personal value is detected. Users must review recordings and exports before saving or sharing them.

Purchases and payment provider

When a customer uses the paid checkout, Lemon Squeezy acts as merchant of record and collects payment, billing, tax, fraud-prevention, order, and license information under its privacy policy. Alfred Labs may receive the customer and order details needed for delivery, licensing, updates, refunds, fraud prevention, accounting, and support. Alfred Labs does not receive or store full payment-card details.

Use, sharing, and retention

Alfred Labs uses contact and order information only to answer requests, deliver and administer licenses, provide requested updates and support, process refunds, protect the product, keep required accounting records, and comply with law. Personal information is not sold.

Email and order records are kept only while reasonably necessary for those purposes or for legally required records, then deleted or anonymized when reasonably possible. Information may be shared with service providers such as GitHub, Google, and Lemon Squeezy only as needed for hosting, email, payment, delivery, or legal compliance.

Choices and privacy requests

Contacting support does not automatically enroll you in marketing. You may request access, correction, deletion, or objection regarding information held directly by Alfred Labs. Some transaction, fraud-prevention, tax, or accounting records may need to be retained. Rights vary by location and applicable law.

Do not send passwords, card details, customer datasets, confidential source code, or other secrets in a support email.

This notice may be updated when the product, website, or service providers change. The effective date on this page will be revised when a change is published.
